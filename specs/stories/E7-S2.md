# Story E7-S2: Audit Trail Recording & Immutability Enforcement

**Epic:** E7 (Admin, Audit & Compliance)  
**Story ID:** E7-S2  
**Title:** Implement audit trail recording and immutability enforcement

---

## Description

The system maintains an append-only audit log of all material operations: claim state transitions, document verifications, fraud evaluations, assessments, settlements, and overrides. Each audit entry includes actor ID, actor role, timestamp (UTC), correlation ID, operation type, and business context. Audit logs are immutable (never modified or deleted) for SOX and regulatory compliance.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **AC1:** AuditLog table schema includes: `audit_id` (PK, UUID), `correlation_id`, `timestamp` (UTC, immutable), `actor_id`, `actor_role`, `operation_type` (enum: CLAIM_CREATED, STATE_TRANSITION, DOCUMENT_VERIFIED, FRAUD_EVALUATED, ASSESSMENT_SUBMITTED, SETTLEMENT_CONFIRMED, OVERRIDE_APPLIED, etc.), `claim_id`, `previous_state` (if applicable), `new_state` (if applicable), `reason_code`, `detail_json` (JSON context).

2. **AC2:** Sensitive data (PII, health info, document URLs) are redacted in audit logs with `[REDACTED]` marker.

3. **AC3:** Every claim state transition triggers an audit log entry automatically (via repository layer).

4. **AC4:** AuditLog records cannot be modified or deleted (database constraints + application-level checks).

5. **AC5:** Queries support filtering by: claim_id, actor_id, operation_type, timestamp range, correlation_id.

6. **AC6:** Endpoint `GET /api/audit-trail/{claim_id}` returns complete audit history for a claim (with pagination), sorted by timestamp ascending.

7. **AC7:** All timestamps are UTC; no timezone conversion. Timestamps are immutable once recorded.
