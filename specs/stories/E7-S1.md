# Story E7-S1: Decision Override Authority & Implementation

**Epic:** E7 (Admin, Audit & Compliance)  
**Story ID:** E7-S1  
**Title:** Implement decision override authority

---

## Description

Admin users can override an AUTO_APPROVE or REJECT decision made by the system/assessor. An override transitions a REJECTED claim to APPROVED (settled) or changes an APPROVED decision to REJECTED. Override requires mandatory reason code, justification comment, and actor ID. The override is recorded immutably in the OverrideAuditLog for compliance and audit.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **AC1:** Service accepts: claim_id, override_decision (APPROVE_REJECTED | REJECT_APPROVED), reason_code (enum: LEGITIMATE_HIGH_VALUE, CLERICAL_ERROR, FRAUD_UNRELATED, etc.), comment (free text), admin_actor_id.

2. **AC2:** Service verifies user has ROLE_ADMIN. If not, it raises AuthorizationError.

3. **AC3:** Service verifies claim is in REJECTED state (for override to APPROVED) or APPROVED state (for override to REJECTED).

4. **AC4:** Service creates immutable OverrideAuditLog entry with: `override_id` (UUID), `claim_id`, `override_type`, `previous_state`, `new_state`, `reason_code`, `comment`, `actor_id`, `actor_email`, `override_at` (UTC, immutable).

5. **AC5:** Service transitions claim state: REJECTED → APPROVED or APPROVED → REJECTED, based on override decision.

6. **AC6:** If override is REJECTED → APPROVED, a settlement is generated (via E5-S2).

7. **AC7:** Override decision is permanent and immutable; no further overrides allowed on the same claim (only one override per claim maximum).

8. **AC8:** OverrideAuditLog entry is immutable after creation (no modification or deletion).
