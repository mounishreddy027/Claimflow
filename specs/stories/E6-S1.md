# Story E6-S1: Dispute Intake & Sub-Claim Generation

**Epic:** E6 (Dispute & Sub-Claim Management)  
**Story ID:** E6-S1  
**Title:** Implement dispute intake and sub-claim generation

---

## Description

When a claimant disputes a settled claim or wants to reopen it for supplemental adjudication, the system creates a linked sub-claim. The sub-claim is a child of the original (parent) claim, inherits the policy and fraud rules from the parent, and enters the FILED state to flow through the pipeline independently. The parent claim remains SETTLED and immutable; the dispute is tracked separately.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **AC1:** Service accepts: parent_claim_id, sub_claim_type (enum: DISPUTE, REOPEN), reason (text), created_by_customer_id.

2. **AC2:** Service verifies parent claim exists and is in SETTLED state. If not, it raises InvalidClaimStateException.

3. **AC3:** Service creates a new SubClaim record with: `sub_claim_id` (UUID), `parent_claim_id`, `sub_claim_type`, `reason`, `created_by_customer_id`, `created_at` (UTC, immutable).

4. **AC4:** Service creates a linked Claim record (child) in FILED state that inherits: `policy_id`, `policy_rule_version_id` (from parent), `line_of_business` (from parent), `customer_id` (from parent).

5. **AC5:** Parent claim remains SETTLED; no state transitions on parent. SubClaim record links parent and child via `parent_claim_id` and `related_claim_id`.

6. **AC6:** Service returns HTTP 201 with sub_claim_id and new child claim_id. Both IDs are included in response for audit tracking.

7. **AC7:** Parent claim is never modified; the relationship is recorded in SubClaim table only.
